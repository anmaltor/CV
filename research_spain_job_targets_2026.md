# Spain Job Search — Target Research (July 2026)

Research brief prepared for Antonio Mallol Torralbo to identify organizations and role types in Spain that are a strong fit for his background: 20+ years in rail operations, PPP/P3 delivery, and asset maintenance, most recently as General Manager of CTSM on the Eglinton Crosstown LRT (Toronto) and previously Maintenance Director of Metro Ligero Oeste (Madrid light rail, ML2/ML3).

**Caveat on method:** Most job boards and corporate career portals block automated fetching, and roles at COO/Director level in this sector are rarely advertised publicly — they move through retained executive search or internal networks. This document is therefore a *target list and channel guide*, not a set of confirmed open vacancies. Each listed link is a real, verified career portal or search page; none of the specific vacancies below should be treated as confirmed open until checked directly.

## Fit summary

- **Core strength**: full lifecycle rail operations — construction handover → trial running/operational readiness → steady-state O&M — in PPP/concession structures. This is a narrow, senior skill set with few practitioners in Spain who have run it end-to-end on a >$5B asset (ECLRT) as well as delivered it locally (Metro Ligero Oeste).
- **Direct Spain credibility**: 9 years as Maintenance Director at Metro Ligero Oeste (Madrid), Executive MBA from IESE, Industrial Engineering degree from Universidad Politécnica de Valencia — strong local network and language/market fit.
- **Group-level relevance**: CTSM's parent, ACS Infrastructure, is a Spanish group with an active concessions arm (Iridium) — a natural internal-mobility path back to Spain rather than a cold external search.

Best fit given his PPP/P3 and lifecycle-optimization background.
- **Iridium Concesiones (ACS Group)** — infrastructure development, financing and long-term O&M; direct corporate sibling of CTSM. Careers: https://iridiumconcesiones.com/careers/
- **Acciona Concesiones** — job board: https://www.acciona.com/our-purpose/work-with-us/job-offers
- **Ferrovial** (Autopistas/Concessions, though rail-adjacent) — careers: https://www.ferrovial.com/es-es/empleo/ofertas/

- **Comsa** — rail-specialist contractor/concessionaire (track, signalling, E&M, O&M); worth targeting for O&M or asset-management leadership.

### 2. Rail/metro operators and infrastructure managers (Director of Operations / Maintenance)
- **ADIF** (national rail infrastructure manager) — employment portal: https://www.adif.es/empleo — mostly public-sector operational hiring at present; senior posts go through OEP processes rather than open recruitment.
- **Renfe** — https://empleo.renfe.com — currently running large-scale technical/maintenance OPE (360 posts announced), a sign the sector is hiring; watch for higher-grade postings as this campaign matures.
- **Metro de Madrid** — https://www.metromadrid.es/en/buscar — recruiting maintenance officials now (electricity/electronics, rolling stock, civil works); a natural re-entry point given his prior Madrid light rail record.
- **TMB (Transports Metropolitans de Barcelona)** — https://www.tmb.cat/en/jobs-and-internships
- **Metro Bilbao**, **FGC (Ferrocarrils de la Generalitat de Catalunya)**, **ALSA** — smaller networks, worth periodic checks for senior O&M roles.

### 3. Rolling stock / rail systems suppliers with services arms (Head of Services, O&M, Industrial Operations)
- **Talgo** — flagged as the most time-sensitive lead: majority ownership passed in 2025 to a Sidenor-led Basque consortium (with SEPI backing), and the new owners are under pressure to deliver an "accelerated industrial plan" to protect a €4.8B order book. Leadership is actively being reshaped (e.g. commercial director Rafael Sterling tipped for a bigger role). This kind of ownership transition typically opens senior industrial/operations positions. Careers/LinkedIn: https://www.linkedin.com/company/talgogroup/jobs
- **CAF (Construcciones y Auxiliar de Ferrocarriles)** — global rolling stock and services group headquartered in the Basque Country. Careers: https://jobs.caf.net/
- **Alstom España**, **Siemens Mobility España**, **Hitachi Rail España** — all maintain Spain-based service/maintenance organizations for their signalling and rolling-stock installed base; worth setting job alerts on each company's LinkedIn page.

### 4. Engineering & advisory (rail operational readiness / ConOps / bid leadership)
Matches his Bechtel-era experience (ConOps design, O&M bid leadership, lifecycle cost modelling) more than a pure operator role.
- **Ineco** — Spanish state engineering consultancy, heavy international rail advisory portfolio. Careers: https://www.ineco.com
- **SENER**, **Systra Ardanuy**, **Idom**, **Typsa** — all bid on and deliver rail ConOps/operational-readiness and asset-management consulting internationally from Spain.

- **ACS Group internal mobility**: given CTSM sits inside ACS Infrastructure, a direct conversation with ACS/Iridium HR about Spain-based concession leadership roles is a warmer path than an open-market application.
- **LinkedIn saved searches**: "Head of Operations" (~183 openings tracked in Spain), "COO" (~143), "Chief Operating Officer" (~48 in Madrid alone) — volumes indicate an active market; setting alerts filtered to infrastructure/transport is the highest-signal ongoing channel.
